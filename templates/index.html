Key Findings & Methodology Insights

1. Gradient Boosting & Random Forest Superiority: Both ensemble architectures achieved over 95% R², capturing non-linear pricing elasticity and seasonal holiday demand variations far better than traditional linear models.
2. Dominant Feature Drivers: Unit_Price (48.0%) and Holiday_Season (16.6%) are the strongest predictors, followed by Product Category and Promotional discounts.
3. Generalization on Unseen Data: Low MAPE of ~9.1% ensures that forecast predictions provide reliable baseline inputs for warehouse stock planning without risking heavy overstocking or stockouts.
